# User Research Plan for Pilot Regions

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the research plan to validate Grayfix's value proposition with regional cooperative partners in pilot regions. The goal is to test core assumptions about agricultural escrow needs, understand regional trust dynamics, and gather feedback to refine the product before scaling.

## Research Objectives

1. **Validate Problem-Solution Fit**: Confirm that the "trust gap" in agricultural trade is a real pain point for target users
2. **Test Core Value Propositions**: Evaluate the appeal of smart escrows, loss-sharing mechanisms, and proof-of-delivery protocols
3. **Understand Regional Variations**: Identify differences in trade practices, dispute resolution preferences, and technology adoption across pilot regions
4. **Gather Usability Feedback**: Test the mobile and web interfaces with actual buyers, sellers, and drivers
5. **Define Success Metrics**: Establish baseline metrics for trade volume, dispute rates, and user satisfaction

## Pilot Scope

### Target Regions

**Phase 1 (Month 1-2)**: 2 pilot regions
- **Region A**: Northern Nigeria (focus on grain trade)
- **Region B**: Southwestern Nigeria (focus on tuber/vegetable trade)

**Phase 2 (Month 3-4)**: Expansion based on Phase 1 learnings

### Target Participants

- **Sellers/Farmers**: 15-20 participants per region (30-40 total)
- **Buyers/Aggregators**: 10-15 participants per region (20-30 total)
- **Drivers/Logistics**: 5-10 participants per region (10-20 total)
- **Cooperative Leaders**: 2-3 per region (4-6 total)

### Trade Volume Target

- **Pilot Goal**: 50-100 trades completed per region (100-200 total)
- **Average Trade Size**: ₦50,000 - ₦200,000 NGN equivalent
- **Duration**: 8 weeks active trading period

## Research Methodology

### 1. Pre-Pilot Stakeholder Interviews (Week 1-2)

**Format**: Semi-structured interviews (45-60 minutes)

**Participants**: Cooperative leaders, prominent buyers/sellers

**Key Questions**:
- What are the biggest pain points in current agricultural trade practices?
- How do you currently handle payment and delivery trust issues?
- What dispute resolution mechanisms exist today?
- What % of trades result in disputes or losses?
- What are the barriers to adopting new technology?
- What payment methods are most trusted?

**Deliverable**: Stakeholder interview summary report

### 2. Onboarding Workshops (Week 3)

**Format**: In-person group sessions (2 hours each)

**Activities**:
- Product demonstration (live walkthrough)
- Wallet setup assistance (Freighter/Albedo)
- Practice trades on testnet
- Q&A and feedback collection

**Deliverable**: Onboarding friction log and participant readiness assessment

### 3. Active Trading Period (Week 4-11)

**Format**: Real trades using Grayfix platform

**Support**:
- WhatsApp support group for each region
- Weekly check-in calls with cooperative leaders
- On-call technical support during business hours

**Data Collection**:
- Transaction logs (on-chain + off-chain metadata)
- Support ticket analysis
- User behavior analytics (app usage patterns)

**Deliverable**: Weekly trade volume reports and issue logs

### 4. Mid-Pilot Feedback Sessions (Week 7)

**Format**: Focus groups (6-8 participants, 90 minutes)

**Participants**: Mixed groups of buyers, sellers, and drivers

**Key Topics**:
- What's working well?
- What's confusing or frustrating?
- Are loss-sharing ratios fair and clear?
- Is proof-of-delivery protocol practical?
- Would you recommend Grayfix to others?

**Deliverable**: Mid-pilot feedback synthesis report

### 5. Post-Pilot In-Depth Interviews (Week 12)

**Format**: Individual interviews (30-45 minutes)

**Participants**: 10-15 active users per region

**Key Questions**:
- Would you continue using Grayfix after the pilot?
- What features are most valuable?
- What features are missing?
- How does Grayfix compare to your previous trade methods?
- What would make you trust the platform more?

**Deliverable**: Post-pilot interview insights report

### 6. Quantitative Survey (Week 12)

**Format**: Online survey (10-15 minutes)

**Participants**: All pilot participants

**Key Metrics**:
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Task completion satisfaction
- Feature importance rankings
- Likelihood to continue using
- Willingness to pay platform fees

**Deliverable**: Quantitative research summary with statistical analysis

## Success Metrics

### Primary Metrics

1. **Trade Completion Rate**: % of initiated trades that complete successfully
   - **Target**: ≥85% completion rate

2. **Dispute Rate**: % of trades that enter dispute resolution
   - **Target**: ≤10% dispute rate

3. **User Retention**: % of users who complete 2+ trades
   - **Target**: ≥60% retention

4. **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Likelihood to recommend Grayfix
   - **Target**: NPS ≥40

### Secondary Metrics

5. **Onboarding Success**: % of participants who successfully complete first trade
   - **Target**: ≥75% onboarding success

6. **Support Ticket Volume**: Average support requests per trade
   - **Target**: ≤0.5 tickets per trade

7. **PoD Adoption**: % of trades using proof-of-delivery video uploads
   - **Target**: ≥50% PoD adoption

8. **Time to Settlement**: Average time from delivery to fund release
   - **Target**: ≤24 hours

9. **Platform Fee Acceptance**: % of users willing to pay 1% platform fee post-pilot
   - **Target**: ≥70% acceptance

## Stakeholders

### Primary Stakeholders

1. **Grayfix Product Team**: Receives research insights for product iteration
2. **Cooperative Leaders**: Gatekeeper relationships, user recruitment partners
3. **Regional Buyers/Sellers**: End users providing feedback
4. **Logistics Partners**: Drivers and transporters testing PoD protocols

### Secondary Stakeholders

5. **Stellar Foundation**: May provide grant funding or technical support
6. **Agricultural NGOs**: Potential distribution partners
7. **Payment Processors**: Integration partners for local currency on-ramps

## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks

- **Wallet Complexity**: Provide white-glove onboarding support and pre-funded testnet wallets
- **Network Connectivity**: Ensure offline-first mobile app capabilities
- **Smart Contract Bugs**: Conduct thorough security audit before pilot launch

### Adoption Risks

- **Low Participation**: Offer incentives (e.g., first 5 trades fee-free)
- **Trust Concerns**: Partner with respected cooperative leaders as champions
- **Language Barriers**: Provide local language support (Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo)

### Operational Risks

- **Dispute Overload**: Have mediator capacity ready (2-3 trained mediators per region)
- **Support Volume**: Set up dedicated WhatsApp support channels with response SLAs
- **Payment Delays**: Ensure liquidity for path payments and monitor Stellar network status
